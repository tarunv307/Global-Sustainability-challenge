THE PROBLEM

India Produces 700+ Million Tonnes of Coal. Subsidence is Detected AFTER the Damage.

When coal is removed from beneath the surface, the ground above slowly sinks — this is subsidence. It cracks homes, farmlands, roads, and water bodies above mine panels across India's coalfields.

Today, subsidence is detected after the damage begins — through manual surveys and periodic inspections. There is no low-cost, real-time, indigenous early warning system. That is the exact gap we aim to close.

700M+ Tonnes coal/year
100+ Coal mine panels
6–12 days Detection delay (current)

AI & PREDICTION

Monitoring Tells You What Happened.
Our AI Tells You What's Coming.

Three AI models work together on the edge hub — running fully offline. No internet needed. No cloud dependency.

01

Anomaly Detection

Flags abnormal tilt, vibration, or displacement instantly. Uses statistical + ML thresholds tuned to each sensor.

02

Multi-Sensor Fusion

Cross-validates all streams — real subsidence shows correlated changes. This eliminates false alarms from vehicles, animals, or rain.

03

LSTM Time-Series Prediction

Predicts the deformation trend and classifies every zone. Gives minutes to hours of warning before visible ground failure.

🟢 SAFE
Normal monitoring. No action required.
🟡 WARNING
Abnormal condition detected. Increase sampling.
🟠 HIGH RISK
Prepare evacuation. Alert control room.
🔴 CRITICAL
Immediate alert. Evacuate affected zone now.
